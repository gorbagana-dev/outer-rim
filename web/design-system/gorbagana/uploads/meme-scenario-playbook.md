# Meme and Scenario Playbook

Use this file for Gorbagiofication, cultural remixes, propaganda, reaction images, quote cards, lore scenes, and concept generation.

## Transformation modes

### A. Gorbagiofication

Pattern: **“Make X into a Gorbagio.”**

Preserve the subject's iconic cues, then convert the anatomy into a reference-supported Gorbagio body family. Fur-plus-can is a useful default, not the only valid anatomy; inspect the asset index before choosing.

Strong cues include:

- hair or head silhouette;
- glasses or facial accessory;
- costume;
- era-specific color/film treatment;
- signature object;
- posture;
- textual attribution or title.

For intellectual/literary figures, use recognizable author-photo cues, era, glasses, coat/suit, cigarette/typewriter/book, documentary grain, etc., while making the creature itself unmistakably Gorbagio.

### B. Famous-scene takeover

Pattern: **“Make the famous [scene/moment] Gorbagana themed.”**

Keep:

- camera angle;
- principal character positions;
- central gesture/action;
- major environmental landmarks;
- emotional timing.

Replace or trashify:

- people -> Gorbagios;
- currencies/resources -> $GOR;
- vehicles -> garbage trucks/dumpsters/scrap machines;
- institutions -> Trash Council, landfill bureaucracy, validators, RPC terminal rooms;
- props -> broken electronics, cans, lids, trash bags, warning stickers;
- signage -> concise Gorbagana copy.

The scene should be recognized before the viewer finishes reading any caption.

### C. Surgical meme edit

Pattern: **“Keep exactly the same but change X.”**

This is not a redesign. Change only X. First confirm that the actual usable image to edit is in the conversation; an inaccessible earlier artifact, filename or URL alone is not the target.

Common examples:

- attribution name;
- ticker;
- sign text;
- face/expression;
- one prop;
- one character replacement.

If the user specifies replacement text, reproduce it exactly.

### D. Quote card / literary meme

Use when a Gorbagio performs a quote, maxim, fake quotation, literary parody, or philosophical meme.

Priorities:

1. quote legibility;
2. Gorbagio character identity;
3. attribution legibility;
4. mood matching the source figure or literary era;
5. one Gorbagana twist.

For parody attributions, do not “correct” the user's pun. Examples of the pattern include transforming a name into a trash/Gorbagio pun while retaining the rest of the card.

### E. Chain propaganda

Useful styles:

- 1930s industrial poster;
- Cold War civil-defense poster;
- municipal sanitation campaign;
- cult recruitment flyer;
- religious tract;
- trash-management public-service announcement;
- photocopied punk zine;
- 1990s rave flyer;
- arcade cabinet attract screen;
- VHS training video;
- fake technical manual;
- bureaucratic seal/document;
- dystopian campaign poster;
- tabloid cover.

Keep the copy punchy. Trash bureaucracy is often funnier than generic crypto triumphalism.

### F. Reaction image

Make the face and body language do most of the work.

Good reaction states:

- euphoric garbage discovery;
- deadpan “this is fine” energy;
- suspicious side-eye;
- deranged conviction;
- enlightened trash philosopher;
- devastated trader;
- smug validator;
- feral 3 a.m. builder;
- bureaucrat denying obvious chaos;
- exhausted bridge operator.

### G. Product / institutional parody

Trashify recognizable categories rather than defaulting to a single brand:

- luxury ad -> garbage-can haute couture;
- bank -> municipal dump treasury;
- exchange -> trash auction window;
- gas station -> $GOR gas depot;
- stock exchange floor -> landfill trading pit;
- corporate boardroom -> Trash Council fluorescent chamber;
- data center -> scavenged server landfill;
- passport/ID -> dumpster citizenship papers;
- central bank note -> garbage scrip.

## Caption formulas

Use sparingly; visual-first is preferred.

For topical satire, prefer a headline that names the subject through a direct Gorbagana pun over an indirect motivational slogan. In the AI data-center battle example, the user preferred “ARTIFICIAL GORBAGE INTELLIGENCE (AGI)” to “KEEP THE TRASH THINKING.” Preserve the intentional spelling “GORBAGE.” Treat this as a model for topical specificity, not mandatory copy for unrelated scenes. Let dialogue carry the secondary situational joke rather than making the headline explain the whole scene.

- “ONE DEV'S TRASH…” / “…IS OUR INFRASTRUCTURE.”
- “BUILD. DUMP. REPEAT.”
- “GAS: $GOR.”
- “THE CHAIN IS TRASH. THAT'S THE POINT.”
- “WELCOME TO THE LANDFILL.”
- “SERIOUS TECH. GARBAGE CIVILIZATION.”
- “NO ROADMAP. CHECK THE DUMPSTER.”
- “TRASH HAS FINALITY.”

Treat these as meme copy, not factual technical claims unless independently verified.

## Composition heuristics

- One Gorbagio close-up + one prop = strongest reaction meme.
- Two characters + one clear conflict = strongest dialogue/scenario meme.
- Three or more characters require stronger staging; do not clutter a square meme.
- For quote cards, reserve 30–45% negative space for text.
- For X banners, place the focal Gorbagio off-center and preserve safe negative space for profile/avatar overlays and mobile crops.
